Tianditu imagery remains © 天地图 / respective data providers. The application supports its documented WMTS image service using the user's own Key: https://lbs.tianditu.gov.cn/server/MapService.html . Tencent imagery remains © 腾讯地图. Its editable compatibility adapter is not an officially documented bulk-download API; access and use follow https://lbs.qq.com/userAgreements/agreements/terms . The official SDK coordinate reference is https://lbs.qq.com/webApi/javascriptGL/glGuide/glBasic . Jilin-1 imagery remains © 吉林一号 / 长光卫星; its getMap service requires the user's assigned TK and layer MK. Provider-published integration reference: https://www.jl1mall.com/forum/PostDetail?postId=202306152108000169890 and the publisher's article https://blog.csdn.net/2301_81507121/article/details/137813697 . Custom endpoints and local DEM files retain their original ownership and license; source attribution is included in exported metadata. An endpoint being technically accessible does not grant additional data rights. No licensed imagery or provider credentials are bundled.

FABDEM V1-2 is approximately 30 m elevation data from University of Bristol under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: https://research-information.bris.ac.uk/en/datasets/fabdem-v1-2/ . Usage of downloaded data follows its provider terms independently of the application's MIT license. No user credentials or licensed map imagery are bundled in the application download.
